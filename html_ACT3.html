Chicken Adobo with Garlic Fried Rice

A classic Filipino comfort meal made with tender chicken simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, and garlic, served with savory garlic fried rice.

Chicken Adobo

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns, and sugar. Add the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Remove chicken from marinade and brown on all sides. Set aside.
  3. Pour the remaining marinade and water into the pan. Bring to a boil.
  4. Return the chicken to the pan, lower the heat, and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes until tender.
  5. Remove the lid and simmer until the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Serve hot with garlic fried rice.

Side Dish: Garlic Fried Rice (Sinangag)

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked day-old rice
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 stalks green onions, chopped (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a wok or large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add minced garlic and sauté until golden brown and fragrant. Be careful not to burn it.
  3. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry for 3 to 5 minutes, breaking up any clumps.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Toss in green onions if using.
  6. Serve immediately alongside the chicken adobo.

Tips

  • For a richer flavor, replace the water with coconut milk.
  • Adobo tastes even better the next day as the flavors deepen.
  • Serve with sliced tomatoes or pickled papaya (atchara) for extra contrast.
